The European Journal of Inorganic Chemistry is proud to showcase a dedicated collection to celebrate the prolific and vital scientific career of Professor Klaus Jurkschat on the occasion of his upcoming 75th birthday. For this, we are partnering with our our Guest Editors: Dr. Alexander Antonov (Universität Regensburg), Prof. Jens Beckmann (Universität Bremen), Prof. Sonja Herres-Pawlis (RWTH Aachen), and Prof. Michael Mehring (Universität Chemnitz). As former mentees, colleagues and collaborators of Klaus, EurJIC and the Guest Editor team are welcoming contributions to this initiative.
